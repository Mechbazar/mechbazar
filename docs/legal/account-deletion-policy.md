## 1. Your Right to Delete

You may request deletion of your [BRAND_NAME] account and the personal data
associated with it at any time. This right is provided under the **Digital
Personal Data Protection Act, 2023**, the **Information Technology (Reasonable
Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information)
Rules, 2011**, and the account-deletion requirements of the Google Play and Apple
App Store developer policies.

Deletion is **free of charge** and does not require you to give a reason.

## 2. How to Delete Your Account

### Option A — In the app (fastest)

1. Open the **[BRAND_NAME]** app
2. Go to **Account**
3. Open **Security & Privacy → Delete Account**
4. Read the summary of what will be deleted and what must be retained
5. Confirm twice — the second prompt is the point of no return
6. Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out

You remain signed in only until you confirm; there is no waiting period and no
further step.

### Option B — On the web

Visit **[WEBSITE_URL]/account-deletion**, enter your registered mobile number,
verify with the OTP, and submit the request. **No app installation is required.**

### Option C — By email

Write to **[DPO_EMAIL]** from your registered email address, or from any address
if you include your registered mobile number, with the subject line
**"Account Deletion Request"**. Include:

- your registered mobile number;
- your full name as registered;
- for Cash-on-Delivery refunds still pending, your bank/UPI details.

We will verify your identity by OTP before acting on the request.

### Option D — By phone

Call **[SUPPORT_PHONE]** during [SUPPORT_HOURS] and ask for account deletion. Our
team will verify your identity and record the request.

## 3. Before You Delete — Please Settle These First

Deletion cannot proceed while any of the following is open. We will tell you what
is blocking the request and hold it until it clears.

- **Active or in-transit Orders** — wait until delivery or cancel the Order.
- **Active or scheduled Service Bookings** — complete or cancel the Booking.
- **Pending returns, replacements or warranty claims** — these close on deletion
  and cannot be revived afterwards.
- **Pending refunds** — a refund cannot be paid to a deleted account. Ensure any
  refund has been credited, or supply bank/UPI details so we can settle it before
  deletion.
- **Outstanding Cash-on-Delivery dues** or any amount payable to a Vendor or
  Mechanic.
- **[BRAND_NAME] credit balance, coupons and loyalty points** — these are
  **forfeited on deletion** and are not convertible to cash. Use them first if
  you wish.
- **Vendor / Mechanic / Delivery Partner accounts** — additional settlement,
  payout reconciliation and statutory KYC retention obligations apply. Contact
  [VENDOR_EMAIL] or [MECHANIC_EMAIL] to begin offboarding.

Once the request is verified and processed, the following are **permanently
deleted or irreversibly anonymised**:

| Data | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Name, gender, date of birth, profile photo | Deleted |

| Mobile number | Deleted from the profile; retained in hashed, non-reversible form only within retained invoice records |

| Device identifiers, push notification tokens (FCM), advertising identifiers | Deleted |

| Login sessions and authentication tokens | Revoked immediately |

Once complete, **you will no longer be able to log in, and your order and booking
history will not be recoverable.** Deletion is **irreversible**.

## 5. What We Must Retain, and Why

Indian law requires certain records to be preserved even after an account is
deleted. These are moved to a **restricted-access archive**, are not used for any
operational, analytical or marketing purpose, and are not linked to a usable
account.

| Retained data | Reason | Period |
|---|---|---|
| Tax invoices, credit notes, order and payment records | Companies Act, 2013 (books of account); CGST Act, 2017 and GST rules; Income-tax Act, 1961 | **8 years** from the end of the relevant financial year |
| Refund, settlement and payout records | Financial audit and reconciliation | **8 years** |
| Partner KYC documents (Vendors, Mechanics, Riders) | Statutory onboarding and tax obligations | **5 years** after the relationship ends |
| Records subject to an ongoing dispute, investigation, claim or legal proceeding | Legal defence and enforcement | Until final resolution, plus the applicable limitation period |
| Fraud, abuse and security records | Prevention of repeat fraud and platform integrity | **[3] years**, in minimised form |
| Server and access logs | IT Act, 2000 and CERT-In directions | **180 days**, unless required for an active investigation |
| Grievance and complaint records | Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 | **3 years** from resolution |
| Record of your deletion request itself | Proof of compliance | **3 years** |

Aggregated and statistical data that cannot identify you — such as
category-level sales volumes — is not personal data and may be retained
indefinitely.

## 6. Timelines

| Stage | Timeline |
|---|---|
| **In-app deletion** — personal data erased, login disabled, push tokens revoked, Firebase sign-in credential removed | **Immediately**, at the moment you confirm |
| Deletion requested by email, phone or web instead of in-app: acknowledgement | **Within 48 hours** |
| Deletion requested by email, phone or web instead of in-app: completion | **Within [30] days** |
| Erasure propagated to encrypted backups | **Within [90] days**, as backups cycle |
| Erasure requests forwarded to processors and marketplace partners | **Within [30] days** |
| Written confirmation of completion | On completion |

**There is no cooling-off period and no way to undo an in-app deletion.** Once
you confirm, the account is gone. This is deliberate — a deletion that lingers
in a reversible state is not a deletion.

Backups are held on a rolling cycle for disaster recovery. Data in a backup is
not restored into production; if a backup is ever restored, deletion requests are
re-applied to it.

## 8. When a Deletion Request May Be Declined or Deferred

We may decline or defer a request where:

a. your **identity cannot be verified** — we will not delete an account on an
   unverified request, since doing so could allow one person to delete another's
   account;
b. there are **open Orders, Bookings, refunds or dues** (Section 3) — the request
   is held, not refused, and processed once these clear;
c. retention is **required by law**, or the data is needed for an ongoing legal
   claim, investigation, or regulatory proceeding;
d. the request is **manifestly frivolous, repetitive or abusive**;
e. deletion would interfere with **fraud prevention** in respect of an account
   under active investigation.

We will always tell you the reason in writing, and you may escalate to the
Grievance Officer.

## 9. Effect of Deletion

- **Immediate:** you are logged out on all devices, cannot log in again, and
  receive no further notifications.
- **Permanent:** order history, bookings, invoices in the app, saved vehicles,
  addresses and preferences are not recoverable.
- **Forfeited:** any [BRAND_NAME] credit balance, promotional credits, coupons and loyalty points
  are cancelled without compensation.
- **Closed:** open returns, replacements and warranty claims are closed and
  cannot be revived.
- **Fresh start:** if you register again with the same mobile number in future,
  it will be a **new account** with no history, and any offer restricted to new
  users may not apply.
