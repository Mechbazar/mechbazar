## What to Include in Your Message

To help us resolve your issue in a single exchange, please include:

- your **registered mobile number**;
- the **Order ID** or **Booking ID**;
- your **vehicle details** — make, model, variant, year — for any fitment issue;
- the **part number** printed on the item, for a wrong-part or defect claim;
- **photographs** of the item, its packaging, its label and the defect;
- the **unboxing video**, if you recorded one, for damage or shortage claims.
