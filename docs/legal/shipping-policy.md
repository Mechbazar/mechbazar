## 1. Scope

This Policy sets out how orders placed on the MechBazar Platform are packed,
dispatched and delivered — including our quick-commerce express delivery, our
standard delivery, applicable charges, tracking, and what happens when a delivery
cannot be completed. It applies to deliveries within India only.

---

## 2. Service Areas

a. We currently deliver in **Information Required** and to serviceable PIN codes
   across India through our logistics partners.
b. Enter your **PIN code** on the product page or at checkout to see whether the
   item is deliverable to your location and which delivery speed is available.
c. Serviceability differs by item. Heavy, bulky or hazardous goods — batteries,
   tyres, lubricants, paints, body panels — may be restricted to particular PIN
   codes or particular carriers.
d. We do not ship outside India, and do not deliver to APO/FPO addresses or to
   locations where access is legally restricted.

## 4. Order Processing

a. Orders are routed to the **Vendor** who listed the item. The Vendor picks,
   packs and hands the shipment to a delivery partner, or — for express
   orders — to a rider dispatched from the nearest fulfilment point or dark
   store.
b. Orders placed after the cut-off time, or on a Sunday or public holiday, are
   processed on the next business day.
c. Multi-item Orders may be **split into multiple shipments** where items are
   held by different Vendors or require different carriers. Each shipment is
   tracked separately, and you are not charged extra for the split.
d. Every shipment carries a **tax invoice** issued by the seller of record,
   showing the description, quantity, HSN code, taxable value, GST and the total.
   Details required by the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 —
   including the name and address of the manufacturer/packer/importer, net
   quantity, retail sale price inclusive of all taxes, month and year of
   manufacture or import, and consumer-care details — are displayed on the
   product listing and on the package as applicable.

## 6. Tracking Your Order

a. Once dispatched, you receive the courier name and tracking number by push
   notification and SMS.
b. Track status in the app under **Orders → [Order] → Track**. Statuses progress
   through *Confirmed → Packed → Dispatched → Out for Delivery → Delivered*.
c. For express orders in supported cities, live rider location and ETA are shown
   on a map during the *Out for Delivery* stage.
d. Tracking events are supplied by the carrier and may take a few hours to
   update. An unchanged status for under 24 hours is normal and usually does not
   indicate a problem.

## 7. Delivery, Handover and Inspection

a. Please ensure that you or an **authorised person aged 18 or above** is
   available at the delivery address during the delivery window.
b. Handover may require a **delivery OTP** or a signature. **Share the delivery
   OTP only after you have physically received the package.**
c. **Inspect before accepting.** If the outer packaging is torn, wet, re-taped,
   opened or visibly tampered with, **refuse the delivery** and report it
   immediately. Accepting a visibly damaged package makes a damage claim
   substantially harder to establish.
d. **Open-box / open-delivery** is available on selected high-value items, where
   indicated on the product page.
e. **Record an unboxing video** for high-value, fragile or electrical parts. For
   claims of missing items, wrong items or transit damage, an unbroken unboxing
   video is the most reliable evidence and materially speeds up resolution.
f. Report any shortage, damage or wrong item **within [48] hours** of delivery
   through **Orders → Report an Issue**, with photographs.

## 8. Cash on Delivery (COD)

a. COD is available on eligible Orders at eligible PIN codes, up to an order
   value cap displayed at checkout.
b. A COD handling fee of **Information Required** may apply.
c. Pay the **exact invoice amount in cash**. Riders may not carry change for
   large denominations. There is no card machine, UPI-on-delivery or any other
   electronic payment option — MechBazar accepts cash only.
d. Always collect a **payment receipt or delivery acknowledgement**.
e. COD may be withdrawn from accounts with a history of repeatedly refused or
   undelivered COD Orders.

## 9. Failed Deliveries, Reattempts and RTO

a. Our delivery partners make up to **3 delivery attempts**. Each attempt is
   preceded by a call or notification.
b. If delivery fails on all attempts — nobody available, address incorrect or
   incomplete, entry refused, phone unreachable, or the recipient declines to
   pay a COD amount — the shipment is **returned to origin (RTO)**.
c. On RTO nothing is charged to you, because payment is collected only at a
   successful delivery. Repeated RTOs on an account may lead to restrictions
   under the [Cancellation Policy](cancellation-policy.md).
d. Deliveries can only be made to the address selected at checkout. **Address
   changes are not possible once the Order is dispatched**; cancel and reorder
   instead, subject to the [Cancellation Policy](cancellation-policy.md).
e. Where a shipment is confirmed **lost in transit**, you receive a full refund
   or a free replacement, at your option.

## 10. Shipment of Restricted and Hazardous Goods

Certain automotive goods are subject to transport, packaging and labelling
restrictions:

- **Lubricants, brake fluid, coolants, paints, adhesives, aerosols** — classified
  as flammable or hazardous; restricted to surface transport, with slower
  timelines and limited PIN-code coverage.
- **Lead-acid batteries** — governed by the Battery Waste Management Rules, 2022.
  Exchange of the old battery may be required at delivery, and a core/exchange
  deposit may apply where the old unit is not handed over.
- **Tyres** — supplied with BIS marking; delivery does not include fitment or
  balancing unless purchased as a service.
- **Airbags, seat-belt pretensioners and pyrotechnic components** — carriage is
  restricted and these may be unavailable for direct-to-consumer delivery.
- **EV batteries and high-voltage components** — subject to specialised handling
  and may be restricted to workshop delivery and professional fitment.

## 11. Doorstep Service Visits

For service bookings, "delivery" means the arrival of the assigned **Mechanic**
at your address:

a. You select a **date and time slot** at booking; arrival within that slot is a
   best-effort estimate.
b. You receive the Mechanic's name, photograph, rating and contact number on
   assignment, and live ETA once they are on the way.
c. Provide safe, lawful and reasonable access to the vehicle and adequate working
   space. Where the vehicle is in a basement, a stack parking bay or a location
   without room to work, the Mechanic may be unable to proceed and a visitation
   charge may apply.
d. If nobody is available at the address, the visit is treated as a
   customer-cancelled booking under the
   [Cancellation Policy](cancellation-policy.md).
