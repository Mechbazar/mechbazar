## 1. Scope

This Refund Policy explains when money you have already paid in cash is
returned to you, how the amount is calculated, how it is paid back, and the
timelines involved. It applies to automotive spare parts, accessories and
consumables purchased through the MechBazar Platform, and to doorstep
mechanic services booked through it.

Read this Policy together with the
[Cancellation Policy](cancellation-policy.md), the
[Return & Replacement Policy](return-replacement-policy.md), the
[Shipping & Delivery Policy](shipping-policy.md) and the
[Terms and Conditions](terms-and-conditions.md).

## 2. When No Refund Arises

In each of the situations below **you have paid nothing**, so there is nothing
to refund and nothing for you to do:

- You cancel an Order before it is dispatched.
- You refuse a delivery at your door.
- An Order is cancelled by a Vendor or by MechBazar before delivery.
- A delivery fails and the shipment is returned to the Vendor.
- You cancel a Service Booking before the Mechanic begins work.
- A Mechanic does not arrive, or a Booking is cancelled by us.

You will receive a cancellation confirmation in the app in each case. **If
anyone contacts you offering a "refund" for one of these situations and asks
for your bank details, card number, UPI PIN or an OTP, it is a fraud attempt.**
See Section 8.

## 3. When a Refund Is Payable

A refund arises only where cash has already changed hands.

### 3.1 Orders for Products

| Situation | Refund |
|---|---|
| Approved return within the **Information Required-day** window (change of mind or incorrect selection by you) | Product value refunded; the delivery charge you paid and any reverse-pickup charge are **not** refunded |
| **Wrong item** delivered and paid for (different part, wrong part number, wrong quantity, wrong brand) | 100% of everything you paid, including delivery charges — or a free replacement at your option |
| **Damaged or defective** item you paid for on delivery | 100% of everything you paid, including delivery charges — or a free replacement at your option |
| **Missing item** discovered after you paid for a multi-item shipment | Full value of the missing item, or dispatch of the missing item |
| **Counterfeit or not-as-described** item | 100% of everything you paid, plus free reverse pick-up |
| You were charged **more than the invoice amount** at the door | The excess, in full |

### 3.2 Service Bookings

| Situation | Refund |
|---|---|
| You paid the Mechanic and the work was not completed as booked | The amount attributable to the uncompleted work |
| You were charged for parts or labour you did not approve | The unapproved amount, in full |
| You paid more than the final invoice | The excess, in full |
| Service **completed** and OTP-confirmed | Not refundable — raise a workmanship claim instead (Section 6) |
| Booking cancelled before work began | Nothing was paid; no refund arises |

## 4. Non-Refundable Items and Charges

The following are **not refundable**:

a. Parts that have been **installed, fitted, used, mounted or attempted to be
   fitted**, including where fitment marks, scratches, grease or torque marks
   are present.
b. **Electrical and electronic components** once installed or bench-tested —
   ECUs, sensors, wiring harnesses, switches, alternators, starter motors,
   ignition coils, infotainment units.
c. **Fluids and consumables** with a broken seal — engine oil, coolant, brake
   fluid, gear oil, additives, paints, adhesives, sprays.
d. **Batteries** where the warranty sticker or terminals show installation, or
   where the electrolyte has been activated.
e. **Made-to-order, custom-cut, special-ordered or imported-on-request** parts.
f. Items returned **without original packaging, manufacturer seal, tags,
   warranty card, invoice or accessories**.
g. Items returned **after the Information Required-day window**.
h. Products listed as **"Non-returnable"**, **"Final sale"** or **"Clearance"**
   on the product page.
i. **Delivery charges** on change-of-mind returns.
j. **Services already performed** and confirmed complete.

## 5. How Refund Amounts Are Calculated

a. Refunds are computed on the **cash amount actually paid by you**, as shown
   on the tax invoice you received at delivery.
b. Where a **coupon or promotional discount** was applied across a multi-item
   Order, the discount is apportioned pro rata and only the net paid value of
   the returned item is refunded. The coupon itself has no cash value and is
   not refunded.
c. Where a return causes the Order to fall below the free-delivery threshold of
   **Information Required**, the delivery charge that was waived may be
   deducted from the refund.
d. **GST** is refunded proportionately with the item value, and a credit note
   is issued by the Vendor in accordance with GST law.
e. A **reverse-logistics charge** may be deducted for change-of-mind and
   incorrect-selection returns. The amount is disclosed before you confirm the
   return, and is **never** levied where the fault is ours or the Vendor's.
f. Deductions may be made for **missing accessories, missing packaging, or
   damage caused after delivery**, up to the value of what is missing or
   damaged, after informing you with photographic evidence.

## 6. How Refunds Are Paid, and When

Because you paid in cash, **there is no original transaction for us to
reverse.** Refunds are therefore paid directly to a bank account or UPI ID that
**you** supply with your return or refund request.

| Stage | Timeline |
|---|---|
| Refund approved (after the returned item passes quality inspection, or the claim is accepted) | Within **[2] business days** |
| Refund credited to your bank account or UPI ID | **5–10 business days** from approval |

**What you need to provide.** Either:

- **Bank transfer** — account holder name (as per bank records), account
  number, and IFSC; or
- **UPI** — a valid UPI ID registered in your own name.

**Important notes.**

- The account **must belong to the person who placed the Order.** We do not pay
  refunds to third-party accounts.
- Refunds delayed or failed because of **incorrect details supplied by you** are
  re-initiated only after you provide corrected details. Please check the
  account number and IFSC carefully.
- Timelines exclude bank holidays and are subject to your bank's processing
  cycles, which are outside our control.
- A refund reference number (UTR) is shared once the refund is initiated. If
  the amount is not visible after the stated timeline, quote the UTR to your
  bank first, then contact us.
- Where you prefer it, and only if you expressly choose it, an approved refund
  may instead be issued as **MechBazar credit** against a future order,
  credited within 24 hours. This is optional — you are always entitled to the
  money instead.

You can track refund status in the app under **Orders → [Order] → Refund
Status**.

## 7. Warranty Claims

Manufacturer or Vendor warranty on a Product is separate from the return
window. A warranty claim is raised through **Orders → [Order] → Raise a
Warranty Claim**, supported by the invoice, photographs and, where relevant, a
mechanic's observation. The warranty period, terms and remedy (repair,
replacement or pro-rata credit) are those declared by the manufacturer or
Vendor on the product page.

Warranty on **workmanship** for doorstep services is **30 days** or **1,000
kilometres**, whichever occurs first, unless a longer period is stated on the
service page. The remedy is re-performance of the work at no cost. Warranty
claims are excluded where the vehicle was subsequently worked on by a third
party, misused, or driven after the fault became evident.

## 8. Fraud Warning and Abuse

### Protect yourself

**MechBazar will never:**

- ask you to pay in advance, online, or by UPI, card, net banking or wallet;
- ask for your card number, CVV, UPI PIN, net-banking password or login OTP;
- ask you to scan a QR code "to receive a refund" — scanning a QR code **sends**
  money, it never receives it;
- ask you to install a screen-sharing, remote-access or "refund helper" app;
- ask you to transfer money to "verify" your account before a refund.

Every genuine refund is initiated by us and lands in the account **you** supplied
through the app. If you are contacted otherwise, report it to support@mechbazar.com
and to the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in, helpline
**1930**).

### Abuse

We may decline a refund, withhold a refund pending investigation, or suspend an
account where we reasonably determine there has been:

- return of a **different, used, damaged or counterfeit** item in place of the
  one delivered;
- a **materially false claim** of non-delivery, damage or defect;
- a pattern of **excessive returns or refused deliveries** inconsistent with
  normal consumer use;
- **resale activity** disguised as retail purchase.

In such cases we will inform you of the reason, and you may escalate to the
Grievance Officer.
