## 5. Purposes of Processing

We process personal data for the following purposes:

1. **Account management** — registering you, verifying your mobile number by OTP,
   authenticating logins, and maintaining your profile, addresses and Garage.
2. **Order and service fulfilment** — accepting orders, routing them to the
   correct vendor, arranging pick-up and last-mile delivery, assigning
   mechanics, and completing jobs at your location.
3. **Fitment and personalisation** — matching parts to your saved vehicles,
   ranking search results, and recommending compatible or frequently
   replaced items.
4. **Payments, invoicing and refunds** — processing payments through our payment
   partners, generating tax invoices, and settling refunds.
5. **Communications** — sending transactional notifications by push, SMS, email,
   WhatsApp or call regarding orders, bookings, deliveries, returns, refunds and
   security. *Transactional messages are integral to the service and cannot be
   opted out of while you hold an active order or booking.*
6. **Marketing** — sending promotional offers, only where you have consented,
   with an unsubscribe or opt-out mechanism in every such message.
7. **Customer support and grievance redressal** — responding to queries,
   investigating complaints, and adjudicating return, replacement and warranty
   claims.
8. **Safety, fraud prevention and platform integrity** — detecting fraudulent
   orders, counterfeit-part listings, COD abuse, refund abuse, multiple-account
   abuse, and misuse of promotional codes.
9. **Analytics and product improvement** — understanding usage, fixing crashes,
   testing features, and improving catalogue accuracy, in aggregated or
   de-identified form wherever feasible.
10. **Legal and regulatory compliance** — maintaining books of account, GST and
    e-invoicing records, responding to lawful requests from government agencies,
    courts and law enforcement, and enforcing our Terms and Conditions.

### Legal bases

We process personal data on the basis of (a) **your consent**, given at the point
of collection; (b) **certain legitimate uses** permitted under the DPDP Act,
including where you voluntarily provide data for a specified purpose; (c) the
**performance of a contract** with you; and (d) **compliance with applicable
law**. Where processing rests solely on consent, you may withdraw that consent at
any time as described in Section 10.

## 9. Security

We implement reasonable security practices and procedures as required by the SPDI
Rules and commensurate with the sensitivity of the data, including:

- encryption in transit (TLS/HTTPS) and encryption at rest for databases and
  file storage;
- OTP-based authentication and signed, expiring session tokens;
- role-based access control, with access to personal data limited to personnel
  who need it for their role;
- masked/proxy telephony for customer–rider and customer–mechanic calls where
  enabled;
- no payment-instrument data of any kind on our systems — because the Platform
  is Cash on Delivery only, there is no card, wallet, UPI or net-banking
  credential to store, lose or breach in the first place;
- refund bank/UPI details held only until the refund is settled, then deleted;
- logging, monitoring, periodic access reviews and vulnerability assessments;
- contractual security obligations on all processors.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. While we work to
protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security. **You are responsible
for keeping your registered mobile number secure and for not sharing your login
OTP with anyone.** MechBazar will never ask you for your OTP, UPI PIN, card
CVV or bank password. If you become aware of any unauthorised use of your
account, notify us immediately at support@mechbazar.com.

In the event of a personal data breach, we will notify the Indian Computer
Emergency Response Team (CERT-In) and, where applicable, the Data Protection
Board of India and affected users, within the timelines prescribed by law.

## 10. Your Rights and Choices

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

1. **Access** — obtain a summary of the personal data we process about you and
   the identities of the entities with whom it has been shared.
2. **Correction and updating** — correct inaccurate or misleading data, and
   complete incomplete data. Profile details, addresses and Garage vehicles can
   be edited directly in the app under **Account**.
3. **Erasure** — request deletion of your personal data and your account. See the
   [Account Deletion Policy](account-deletion-policy.md).
4. **Withdraw consent** — withdraw consent for any processing based on consent.
   Withdrawal is not retrospective and may prevent us from providing parts of the
   service.
5. **Opt out of marketing** — unsubscribe from promotional email, disable
   promotional push notifications in *Account → Notifications*, or reply STOP to
   promotional SMS. Transactional messages will continue while you hold an
   active order or booking.
6. **Grievance redressal** — escalate any concern to our Grievance Officer
   (Section 14).
7. **Nominate** — nominate another individual to exercise your rights in the
   event of your death or incapacity, as provided under the DPDP Act.

To exercise any of these rights, write to **Information Required** from your registered
email address or contact number. We may ask you to verify your identity before
acting on a request, and will respond within **30 days**.

You also have a corresponding duty under the DPDP Act not to furnish false
particulars, impersonate another person, or file frivolous or false grievances.
