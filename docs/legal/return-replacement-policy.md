## 1. Scope

This Policy governs the return and replacement of automotive spare parts,
accessories and consumables purchased through the MechBazar Platform. Refund
amounts and timelines are governed by the [Refund Policy](refund-policy.md);
cancellations before delivery are governed by the
[Cancellation Policy](cancellation-policy.md).

Automotive parts are a category where a single wrong fitment attempt can render
an item unsaleable. The conditions below reflect that, while preserving every
right available to you as a consumer under the **Consumer Protection Act, 2019**.

## 2. Return Window

You may raise a return request **within Information Required days of delivery**,
subject to the eligibility conditions in Section 3.

| Reason for return | Window |
|---|---|
| Wrong item, wrong part number, wrong quantity, wrong brand | **[48] hours** from delivery |
| Damaged in transit | **[48] hours** from delivery |
| Missing item in a shipment | **[48] hours** from delivery |
| Item not as described / counterfeit | **Information Required days** |
| Defective on arrival (DOA) | **Information Required days**, or the manufacturer's DOA window if longer |
| Change of mind / incorrect selection by you | **Information Required days** |

The short window for transit damage, shortages and wrong items exists because
these claims must be raised with the carrier and the Vendor while evidence is
still available. **Report them the same day wherever possible.**

## 3. Eligibility Conditions

A returned item is accepted only if **all** of the following are true:

a. It is **unused, uninstalled and unfitted** — free of fitment marks, scratches,
   grease, oil, torque marks, cut cable ties, removed protective film or
   evidence of trial fitting.
b. It is in its **original manufacturer packaging**, with the box undamaged and
   all seals, stickers, warranty cards, manuals, mounting hardware, fasteners,
   gaskets and free accessories included.
c. The **serial number, batch code, part number, QR code and security seal** match
   those dispatched.
d. The **original tax invoice** is available.
e. The return request is raised **within the applicable window**.
f. The item is not listed under Section 4.

## 4. Non-Returnable Items

The following are **not eligible for return**, except where the item is proven
defective, counterfeit or materially not as described:

1. **Installed or fitted parts of any kind**, including where the fitment
   attempt was unsuccessful.
2. **Electrical and electronic components** once installed or bench-tested — ECUs,
   engine and body control modules, sensors, wiring harnesses, relays, switches,
   alternators, starter motors, ignition coils, fuel pumps, infotainment units,
   reverse cameras.
3. **Fluids and consumables with a broken seal** — engine oil, gear oil, coolant,
   brake fluid, power-steering fluid, AdBlue/DEF, additives, greases, paints,
   primers, adhesives, sealants, aerosols.
4. **Batteries** where the warranty sticker is removed, the terminals show
   installation marks, or the electrolyte has been activated.
5. **Tyres and tubes** that have been mounted, fitted or driven on.
6. **Made-to-order, custom-cut, custom-painted, special-order or
   imported-on-request** parts.
7. **Cut-to-length goods** — hoses, wiring, trims, mats, upholstery.
8. **Safety-critical components with broken seals** — airbags, seat-belt
   pretensioners, pyrotechnic units.
9. Items sold as **"Non-returnable"**, **"Final sale"**, **"Clearance"**,
   **"As-is"**, **"Open box"** or **"Scratch and dent"**, where clearly marked on
   the product page before purchase.
10. **Used, refurbished or salvage parts** sold expressly on a no-return basis, as
    disclosed on the listing.
11. Items damaged by **misuse, incorrect installation, overloading, accident,
    negligence or unauthorised modification** after delivery.
12. Items with **removed, defaced or altered** serial numbers, batch codes or
    manufacturer markings.

Non-returnable status is displayed on the product page **before** you place the
Order. Nothing in this Section affects your statutory rights in respect of goods
that are defective, unsafe, counterfeit or not as described.

## 5. Fitment Errors and Vehicle Compatibility

The Platform provides fitment filtering from the vehicle details saved in your
**Garage** and from OEM part numbers. These are aids, not guarantees — fitment
varies with facelifts, mid-cycle manufacturer changes, regional variants and
prior repairs.

| Cause of the mismatch | Treatment |
|---|---|
| **Your vehicle details were entered incorrectly**, or you selected a part not matching your variant | **Incorrect-selection return.** Product value refunded; outbound delivery, express and COD fees, and a reverse-pickup charge, are not refunded |
| **Our catalogue data or fitment mapping was wrong** for the vehicle you correctly specified | **Our error.** Full refund including all charges, or free replacement, with free reverse pick-up |
| **Vendor dispatched a different part** from the one ordered | **Vendor error.** Full refund including all charges, or free replacement, with free reverse pick-up |

**Before fitting any part, compare the part number and physical form against the
component being replaced.** Once a part has been fitted or attempted to be
fitted, it becomes non-returnable even where the mismatch was not your fault —
in that case the remedy is a replacement or warranty claim rather than a return.

## 6. Replacement

Where you prefer a replacement over a refund, we will dispatch the correct or
undamaged item at no additional cost, subject to availability.

a. Replacement is available for **wrong item, damaged in transit, defective on
   arrival, and not-as-described** claims.
b. **Free replacement** is provided where the fault lies with us, the Vendor or
   the carrier — including free reverse pick-up.
c. Replacement is **like-for-like** — the same part number, brand and
   specification. Where the exact item is unavailable, we will offer an
   equivalent or better substitute for your approval, or a full refund.
d. Replacements are dispatched **within [2] business days** of the returned item
   passing quality inspection, or immediately on approval where we waive
   inspection.
e. A replacement carries the **remainder of the original warranty**, not a fresh
   warranty period, unless the manufacturer states otherwise.
f. **One replacement per item** is generally permitted. If the replacement is also
   unsatisfactory, the remedy is a full refund.

## 7. How to Raise a Return or Replacement Request

1. Open the app → **Orders**
2. Select the Order → **Return / Replace**
3. Select the item, the quantity and the reason
4. **Upload photographs** (mandatory for damage, defect, wrong item and shortage
   claims):
   - the outer shipping box, showing the label and any damage
   - the product with its packaging, seals and stickers
   - a close-up of the **part number** and any serial or batch code
   - the specific defect or damage
   - the unboxing video, if you recorded one
5. Choose **Refund** or **Replacement**
6. For COD Orders, enter **bank account or UPI details** for the refund
7. Confirm the pick-up address and slot

Alternatively, contact **support@mechbazar.com** or **+91 9772704981**
(Information Required) with your Order ID.

**Do not ship an item back on your own initiative.** Self-shipped returns without
an approved return authorisation cannot be tracked to your Order and will not be
processed or reimbursed.

## 8. Reverse Pick-Up

a. Once a return is approved, a **reverse pick-up** is scheduled within
   **[2–4] business days** at serviceable PIN codes.
b. Pack the item in its **original packaging inside an outer box**. Do not affix
   tape or labels directly to the manufacturer's box.
c. Enclose the **invoice copy** and the return authorisation reference.
d. Hand over **all** accessories, hardware and free items received with the
   product. Missing components are deducted from the refund.
e. **Up to [2] pick-up attempts** are made. If both fail because nobody is
   available, a **₹200 no-show fee** applies and the return request is closed
   and must be raised afresh, subject to the window still being open.
f. Where reverse pick-up is **not available** at your PIN code, we will authorise
   a **self-ship** return and reimburse reasonable courier charges — up to
   **₹[200]**, against a receipt — where the fault is ours or the Vendor's.
g. **Reverse-logistics charges** may be deducted for change-of-mind and
   incorrect-selection returns. The amount is disclosed before you confirm the
   return. No such charge is levied where the fault is ours or the Vendor's.

## 9. Quality Inspection

a. Returned items undergo a **quality check** at the Vendor's or our facility
   within **[2] business days** of receipt.
b. The check verifies condition, completeness, part number, serial/batch code and
   seal integrity against what was dispatched.
c. **If the item passes**, the refund or replacement is processed immediately.
d. **If the item fails** — it is used, fitted, damaged after delivery,
   incomplete, or is a different item from the one dispatched — the return is
   rejected. We will inform you with photographic evidence, and the item is
   returned to your address at no charge to you. Deliberate substitution of a
   different item is treated as fraud under Section 11.
e. You may contest a failed inspection by writing to Information Required within
   **[7] days** of being notified.

## 10. Warranty Claims (After the Return Window)

A manufacturer or Vendor warranty operates **separately from and beyond** the
Information Required-day return window.

a. The warranty period and terms are those declared by the manufacturer or Vendor
   on the product page.
b. Raise a claim through **Orders → [Order] → Raise a Warranty Claim**, with the
   invoice, photographs, odometer reading and — where relevant — a fitment record
   or mechanic's observation.
c. The remedy under warranty is determined by the manufacturer or Vendor: repair,
   replacement, or pro-rata credit.
d. Warranty is typically **void** where the part was incorrectly installed,
   installed by an unqualified person where professional fitment was specified,
   used in racing or commercial overloading, or where the vehicle continued to be
   operated after the fault became evident.
e. Some manufacturers require the failed part to be surrendered for inspection
   before a claim is settled. Turnaround in such cases follows the
   manufacturer's process and may extend beyond our standard timelines.

**Workmanship warranty** on doorstep services is **30 days** or **1,000
kilometres**, whichever occurs first, unless a longer period is stated on the
service page. The remedy is re-performance at no cost.

## 11. Fraud and Abuse

We may reject a return, withhold a refund, or suspend an account where we
reasonably determine that:

- a **different, used, damaged, counterfeit or empty** item was returned in place
  of the one delivered;
- a claim of damage, shortage or non-delivery is **materially false**;
- the item was **used and then returned** as unused;
- there is a pattern of **excessive returns** inconsistent with normal consumer
  use;
- the account is being used for **resale** disguised as retail purchase.

We will inform you of the reason and the evidence, and you may escalate to the
Grievance Officer. Serious cases may be reported to law enforcement.
