## Who We Are

**MechBazar** is a quick-commerce marketplace for automotive spare parts and
doorstep mechanic services, built for car and two-wheeler owners across India. It
is owned and operated by **MECH BAZAR PRIVATE LIMITED** (CIN: U45300HR2026PTC148525), a company
incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, with its registered office at
Sector 70A Extension Road, Gurugram, Haryana, India.

We exist because buying a car or bike part in India is harder than it should be.
The right part for your exact variant is often two markets away, the price
depends on who is asking, and the difference between a genuine component and a
convincing counterfeit is not visible across a counter. Meanwhile, a routine
service means surrendering your vehicle for a day.

MechBazar replaces that with something simpler: **tell us your vehicle, and we
show you only the parts that fit — then deliver them, or send a verified mechanic
to your door.**

## What We Do

### Genuine parts, filtered to your vehicle

Save your car or bike in your **Garage** — make, model, variant, fuel type and
year — and the catalogue reshapes itself around it. Engine and filtration parts,
brakes and suspension, batteries and electricals, lighting, body and trim,
lubricants, tyres, accessories and tools. Every listing shows the OEM or OE part
number, the brand, and whether the item is Genuine/OEM, OES or aftermarket, so
you know exactly what you are buying before you buy it.

### Quick commerce for parts

Vehicles break down at inconvenient times. In our serviced cities, eligible
in-stock parts are delivered in **Information Required**, with live rider
tracking. Everywhere else, standard delivery runs at
**Information Required** through our logistics partners.

### Doorstep mechanic services

Book periodic servicing, diagnostics, brake and battery work, AC service, or
roadside assistance — performed at your home or office by a **verified mechanic**,
in a slot you choose. You see the mechanic's name, photograph, rating and live
ETA before they arrive, approve any additional work before it starts, and confirm
completion with an OTP.

### One place to manage it all

Track every order and booking in real time, keep multiple vehicles in your
Garage, store delivery and service addresses, retrieve every invoice, and raise a
return, replacement or warranty claim from the order itself.

## How the Marketplace Works

MechBazar is a **marketplace**. Parts are listed and fulfilled by independent
**Vendors**, and services are performed by independent **Mechanics**. Our role is
to verify them, standardise the catalogue, hold them to a service standard, and
stand behind your experience.

- **Vendors** are onboarded only after document and GST verification, and their
  listings are reviewed before going live. Performance, cancellation rates and
  return rates are monitored continuously.
- **Mechanics** are onboarded after identity verification, skill assessment and
  background checks, and are rated by customers after every job.
- **Delivery partners** are verified and, in supported cities, connected to you
  through masked calling that protects both parties' numbers.

## What We Stand For

**Genuine parts, plainly labelled.** Counterfeit automotive parts are a road
safety problem, not merely a commercial one. Every listing declares its
provenance — Genuine/OEM, OES, aftermarket or remanufactured — and counterfeit
listings are removed and the vendor delisted.

**Fitment accuracy over catalogue size.** A larger catalogue is worthless if it
sells you a part that does not fit. We invest in vehicle-to-part mapping so the
default answer is the right one.

**Transparent pricing.** The price you see includes GST. Delivery, express and
COD handling charges are shown before you pay, never after.

**Fair, fast redressal.** Wrong part, damaged part, or a job not done right — we
resolve it within published timelines, with the escalation path stated openly in
our [Contact Us](contact-us.md) page.

**Safety first.** We publish honest guidance about what should not be attempted
at the roadside, and our mechanics decline work that cannot be done safely
outside a workshop.
